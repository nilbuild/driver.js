Each recipe is one job, with a live demo where it helps and the code to copy. The guides explain the features; the recipes put them together.

## Onboard New Users

- [First-Run Onboarding](/docs/first-run-onboarding): show a tour once on the first visit, with a replay button.
- [Getting-Started Checklist](/docs/getting-started-checklist): a checklist whose items each launch a short tour.
- [Announce a Feature](/docs/announce-a-feature): point out something new once, or with a hint that stays dismissed.
- [Start From a Help Menu](/docs/start-from-a-help-menu): "Take the tour" starts at the step for the current screen.

## Teach a Task

- [Teach a Form](/docs/teach-a-form): explain each field, or move on only when a field is valid.
- [Walk Through a Menu](/docs/walk-through-a-menu): open a dropdown, pick an item, recover when it closes.
- [Steps Inside a Dialog](/docs/steps-inside-a-dialog): run steps inside a modal and come back to the page.
- [Wait for a Save](/docs/wait-for-a-save): hold a step until your app finishes, then show it's done.
- [Right-Click and Double-Click Steps](/docs/right-click-and-double-click): accept only the gesture the step teaches.

## Keep Progress

- [Resume a Tour](/docs/resume-a-tour): pick up at the step where the user left.
- [Multi-Page Tours](/docs/multi-page-tours): continue a tour after a page load or route change.

## Control How a Tour Ends

- [Confirm Before Closing](/docs/confirm-before-closing): ask before an early exit, or don't allow one.
- [Track Tour Analytics](/docs/track-tour-analytics): finished or closed, and the step where people quit.

## Fit Your App

- [React and Vue](/docs/react-and-vue): set up and clean up in React, Vue and Next.js.
- [Custom Buttons](/docs/custom-buttons): add Skip and Restart, rename and translate buttons.
- [Match Your Brand](/docs/match-your-brand): dark and brand themes with a class and CSS variables.
- [Build Your Own Tour UI](/docs/build-your-own-tour-ui): a checklist panel, a chat assistant, a custom spotlight and form hints, all headless.
